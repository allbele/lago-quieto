## Title
Lago Quieto (Quiet Lake)

## Short description (≤ 300 characters)
A zen clicker with no text, no score and no losing. Toss pebbles into a lake at night and watch it wake up: fireflies, fish, a rising moon, the mist lifting. The lake stays alive after you leave. Steam version adds achievements, extra themes, cloud saves and a wallpaper mode.

## About this game (Steam BBCode)

[h2]Throw a stone. The lake does the rest.[/h2]

You are sitting on the shore of a lake at night, tossing pebbles into the water for no reason at all. Every ripple wakes the lake a little more: fireflies arrive, a fish comes up to look, the moon rises, the mist drifts away.

[b]There is no goal, no score and no way to lose.[/b] The game never asks anything of you — it only answers. The longer you stay, the more alive the lake becomes. And it keeps living after you close the window: when you return, a cascade of rings tells you what happened while you were gone.

[h3]What you'll find[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Every click is a note.[/b] Rings on the water play a soft scale; clicks in sequence become accidental melodies.
[*][b]A lake that wakes up slowly.[/b] Fireflies, reeds, fish, blooming lilies, a frog, the moon, the mist, the aurora and, at last, the dawn.
[*][b]Ambient mode.[/b] Leave it open: the UI fades, the cursor disappears and the lake becomes a living soundscape.
[*][b]100% synthesized audio.[/b] No samples — every plop, cricket and note is generated in real time.
[*][b]Zero on-screen text.[/b] No tutorial, no numbers, no notifications. Just icons and water.
[/list]
